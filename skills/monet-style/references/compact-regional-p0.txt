SKY/MIST: broad connected marks. WATER: broken horizontal strokes shaped by ripples and reflections, highlights only where light demands. GRASS/FOLIAGE: varied directional clusters with gaps. FABRIC/FIGURES: longer marks following folds and wind. BUILDINGS/GRAINSTACKS: coherent light/shadow masses and material-aligned marks. Vary mark scale with depth; avoid tiled micro-dabs and global blur.
